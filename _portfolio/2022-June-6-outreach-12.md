Outreach event based on educational efforts proposed in my NSF CAREER proposal and supported by our NSF REU Site. Encouraged REU students from across the U.S. to engage in outreach work by mentoring high school students from historically marginalized backgrounds on block-based coding to understand computational thinking, build a Lego robot with expanding sensor payloads, and apply their skills to maze navigation to demonstrate their learning objectives.
