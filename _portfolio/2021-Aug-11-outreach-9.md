Outreach workshop to 6 middle school science teachers from Lincoln Public Schools presenting data from my NSF CAREER award and our NSF NRI project on Leveraging Environmental Monitoring UAS in Rainforests.

This workshop will focus on presenting important, highly interdisciplinary research findings to allow middle school teachers to present the scientific method in the context of ongoing, cutting edge scientific exploration. A significant portion of this workshop will be discussion-based to allow for engagement with the topic area and to steer the focus of the course based on the interests of the teachers.
