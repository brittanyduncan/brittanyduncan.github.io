Hosted a group of 30 middle school students and their teachers to explain our work on field robotics and human-drone interaction. Supported by our ongoing NSF CAREER and NSF National Robotics Initiative funding. Demonstrated ongoing, cutting-edge science to the students, discussed pathways and courses of interest to pursue robotics, and gave opportunities to undergraduate students to present their work.
