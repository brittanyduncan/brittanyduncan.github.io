Presented on my path to academia, how my interests drove my research, and why high school students should consider computing careers in the future. Focused discussion on how serendipitous interactions and opportunities allow me to develop flying robots to help people accomplish their jobs in novel, safer ways so that scientists can better monitor the environment.
